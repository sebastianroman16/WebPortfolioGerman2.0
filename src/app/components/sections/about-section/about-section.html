About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering student with a strong hands-on background in electrical systems, industrial automation, and control. I began my technical career in Santiago, Chile, where I trained as an electrical technician and gained early industry experience through an internship that led to my first professional role.

I later earned an associate’s degree in Automation and Industrial Control, graduating as valedictorian, and worked in industrial and manufacturing environments performing troubleshooting and electrical and control systems implementation. After relocating to the United States in 2018, I continued my professional work in electrical and control systems while completing an HVAC program, obtaining my professional license, and working as an HVAC technician. I was subsequently offered an instructor position at the same technical school where I obtained my certification, where I taught electrical and HVAC fundamentals.

I am currently a Student Assistant at the University of South Florida, supporting the Automation and Controls Laboratory, assisting with laboratory-based courses, and presenting laboratory capabilities during university events and sponsor visits. I was selected for this role following a research project in which I built, commissioned, and brought into operation the first delta robot at USF (Omron Quattro 650HS).

I am completing my Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering at the University of South Florida, with an expected graduation date of December 2026. I am passionate about electrical engineering and seek opportunities to apply and further develop my technical skills within a professional engineering environment, contributing to practical solutions while continuing to grow as an engineer.
